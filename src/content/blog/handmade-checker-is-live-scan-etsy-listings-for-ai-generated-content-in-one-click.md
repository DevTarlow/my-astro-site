This started when I was shopping on Etsy for a gift. I wanted something actually handmade, and after scrolling through pages of listings (especially in categories like "seeds"), I started noticing the same pattern over and over.

![Checking Etsy AI Listing](/images/checking-ai-listing-etsy.png)

The descriptions all had the same structure and pacing, and the product photos had the exact telltale artifacts you see in AI-generated images. More and more listings are using AI to fake a handmade look without anyone actually making anything by hand, and it gets harder for shoppers to tell the difference.

So I built a Chrome extension to help check listings quickly.

## What it does

Handmade Checker analyzes Etsy listings using Google Gemini and gives you a quick read on whether a product looks handmade or AI-generated. It is free to start: just create an account and you can scan any listing in one click.

![Handmade Checker Screenshot](/images/handmade-checker-screenshot.png)

Here is how it works:

1. Browse Etsy normally and open any listing page.
2. Click "Check Listing" right below the "Add to Cart" button.
3. Get a 0-100% score with color-coded results (green for likely handmade, orange or red for likely AI-generated).
4. Read the breakdown showing why it scored that way across images, title patterns, and description signals.

No copying links or pasting text into another tab.

## Why it matters

People go to Etsy expecting real handmade goods. When AI-generated listings bury actual makers, buyers lose trust in what they are ordering and real sellers have a harder time competing with automated volume. Handmade Checker won't fix Etsy on its own, but it gives shoppers a fast way to double-check a listing before buying.
