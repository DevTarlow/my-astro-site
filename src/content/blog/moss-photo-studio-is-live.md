[Moss Photo Studio](https://mossphotostudio.com/) is officially live, and honestly it feels really good to finally get this out the door.

It took a long stretch of trial and error to reach this version. Earlier this year I was shipping a lot of smaller tools quickly: [CopySprout](/projects/copy-sprout/) for Etsy copy, [Handmade Checker](/projects/handmade-checker/) for spotting AI listings, and [Moss Toolbox](/projects/moss-toolbox/) as a shared home for business utilities. Putting things out fast felt exciting after coming back from a few years away from software, until I realized I was spreading myself too thin across half a dozen repos and asking sellers to piece together tools that really belonged in one place.

## The process to get here

When I stepped back and retired CopySprout and paused the old Moss Toolbox setup, a lot of the reason was simple: I wanted fewer projects so I could actually give the good ones a real second and third pass.

Looking at how people were using those earlier tools, the same bottleneck kept showing up. Most independent sellers already know what they make and why someone would want it. The hard part is turning one regular phone photo taken on a table or near a window into a full listing that looks ready for Etsy, Shopify, eBay, or TikTok Shop without paying for a studio shoot or fighting a generic image prompt that changes your actual product.

If you drop a mug or a candle into a standard AI generator and ask for a lifestyle shot, half the time it comes back with a slightly different handle, altered glaze, or garbled label text. For someone selling a real physical item, that makes the photo unusable.

So I rebuilt around that single job. Instead of throwing twenty unrelated widgets into a toolbox, I spent the time tightening the photo read, keeping product shape and labels consistent across scenes, and cleaning up every step of the flow until it felt calm and straightforward to use.

## What is live right now

![Four tools in one studio inside Moss Photo Studio](/images/moss-photo-studio.png)

[Moss Photo Studio](https://mossphotostudio.com/) puts four tools under one account and one credit balance:

- **Set Builder** takes one product photo, reads it and plans a shot list for free (0 credits), and lets you generate only the frames you pick at 1 credit each, then download individual shots or a `.zip` set.
- **AI Image Editor** opens any existing photo or generated frame and changes the background, lighting, surface, or puts the item on a model from templates or plain-English directions.
- **AI Image Generator** creates up to four new scenes from a description or a saved item, and sends any result straight into the editor if you want to adjust it.
- **Listing Writer** uses the same photo read to draft three titles, a description, key points, and tags sized for Etsy, Shopify, eBay, Poshmark, Depop, Vinted, TikTok Shop, or your own site.

New accounts start with 10 free credits with no card required, and one-time credit packs start at $12 for 25 credits. Reading a photo and planning shots is free, every generation costs 1 credit, failed runs refund automatically, and credits never expire. There is also a built-in library of 100 starter products, 50 studio people, and 50 scenes so you can test ideas right away, plus free saving for your own products, people, scenes, and brand colors.

It still pays to double-check finished shots before publishing (tiny packaging text or odd reflections can still slip occasionally, so you can remake any frame from the same source photo). Getting those guardrails right took just as much work as the UI itself.

## Proud to share it, and looking ahead

Getting to launch day took a lot of late sessions, rewriting pages that felt cluttered, and throwing out parts that got in the user's way. I am genuinely proud and happy to be sharing something this useful after putting that kind of care into it.

Now that it is live, the real work starts. I am looking forward to making improvements as people use it, adding new features based on what sellers run into day to day, and keeping pricing and limits simple so people can create freely and put their products in front of more buyers without another monthly subscription standing in the way.

If you want a closer breakdown of how the app is built, I wrote up the [Moss Photo Studio project page](/projects/moss-photo-studio/), or you can jump in with 10 free credits at [mossphotostudio.com](https://mossphotostudio.com/).
