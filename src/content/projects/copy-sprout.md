Writing is the hardest part of selling handmade things. Product descriptions, renewals, shop announcements, social posts. Every word of it is a wall for someone who would rather be at the workbench.

CopySprout is my AI writing tool for Etsy sellers, built to sound like a real person instead of a marketing brochure. Sixteen tools for the everyday parts of running a shop.

Here's how it works:

1. **Pick a tool.** Product description, renewal, shop announcement, whatever you're working on.
2. **Answer a few questions.** What do you make, who is it for, what should it say. No marketing speak required.
3. **Get writing you can edit.** It comes back ready to paste or tweak.
4. **Try again if it's not right.** One click, a fresh take.

No jargon, no content strategy, no tone sliders. Just writing you can use.

CopySprout is a subscription, on purpose. Every creation costs me money, and a subscription is what keeps the product running and improving. It only works if it keeps earning its place every month.

- **7-day free trial** - 100 creations, card at checkout, cancels anytime
- **$4.99/month** - 500 creations a month
- **$49.99/year** - two months free
