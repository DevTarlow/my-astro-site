**CopySprout has been discontinued.** The app is no longer online, though I left this page up because building it shaped how I approach writing tools.

Writing copy is usually the hardest part of selling handmade products. Product descriptions, renewals, shop announcements, and social posts all take time away from actually making things.

CopySprout gave Etsy sellers sixteen simple tools designed to write plain, believable copy that sounded like a shop owner instead of an ad agency.

Here is how it worked:

1. Pick a tool (product description, renewal, shop announcement, or review reply).
2. Answer a few plain questions about what you make, who buys it, and what to mention.
3. Get a draft ready to paste or edit, or click once to try another take.

No marketing jargon or complicated sliders. Because every generation called a hosted model and cost money per request, CopySprout ran on a subscription rather than a one-time license.

I wrote the full backstory in [How I Built CopySprout](/blog/how-i-built-copysprout-an-ai-writing-tool-for-etsy-sellers/) and covered why I retired it in [A Few Repos at a Time](/blog/a-few-repos-at-a-time/).
